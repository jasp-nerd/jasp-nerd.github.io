Hey, I'm Jasper. I'm an AI student at VU Amsterdam and currently working as a Working Student in AI & Data Science at Siemens, where I develop AI-powered solutions for building service operations.

I'm also a Student Assistant at the [VU Education Lab](https://vu.nl/en/education/vu-education-lab), where I deliver AI workshops to 200+ faculty and built a [Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/vu-education-lab-ai-assis/mehgkempbebagedafdmlkojjlmkbcgno) that turns web content into educational resources. I also serve as an [AISO](https://www.aisoamsterdam.com/) AI Impact Ambassador and sit on the VU Honours Evaluation Committee.

Most of my projects come from things I run into at uni or problems I want to solve for myself: a tool to bulk-download Canvas courses, scrapers for apartment hunting in Amsterdam, ML models for detecting AI-generated text. I like building things that are actually useful.

When I'm not coding I'm probably walking around Amsterdam with music on.
